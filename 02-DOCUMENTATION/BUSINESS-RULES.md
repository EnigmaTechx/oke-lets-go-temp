# Locked Business / Operations Rules

These rules were defined for the OK Let's Go concept and should be treated as product requirements unless the owner explicitly changes them.

## Districts
- Toronto
- Waterloo
- Kitchener

## Waterloo–Kitchener pricing
- Distance price starts at $1.00/km at 1 km.
- It increases evenly to $1.25/km at 30 km.
- At 30 km and above it stays $1.25/km.
- Customer minimum fare: $7.50.

## Toronto pricing
- $1.25/km from the first kilometre.
- Slow traffic: $0.35/minute when speed is below 15 km/h after pickup.
- Slow-traffic charge stops when speed reaches 15 km/h or higher.
- Customer minimum fare: $7.50.

## Driver minimum and splits
- Driver guaranteed minimum payout: $6.00 per ride.
- Fare $7.50–$10.00:
  - Driver: $6.00
  - District manager: 10%
  - Remaining amount: OK Let's Go
- Fare over $10.00:
  - Driver: 60%
  - OK Let's Go: 30%
  - District manager: 10%

## Arrival / waiting
- 3-minute free grace period.
- Then $0.50/minute until trip start.

## Customer cancellation
- Free within first 2 minutes.
- After 2 minutes while driver is en route: $5.00.
- After driver arrives: $7.50.
- No-show after grace period: $7.50 plus waiting charges.
- Driver cancellation: no customer fee.

## Extra stops
- $4.00 per extra stop.
- Waiting at extra stop: $0.20/minute from the start of the stop.
- No free waiting time at an extra stop.
- After 5 minutes the driver may end or continue.

## Scheduled rides
- No surcharge.
- Visible 24 hours before pickup.
- Driver may claim.
- Driver must confirm 60 minutes before pickup.
- If not confirmed, ride is re-offered.
- Recheck around 30 minutes before pickup.

## On-demand assignment
- Closest qualified driver plus reliability factor.
- 15-second offer.
- Expand search radius if needed.
- Before accepting, driver sees ETA and distance.

## Reliability
Starts at 100 points.
- Cancel after accepting: -5
- Late cancel on scheduled ride: -10
- Driver no-show: -15
- Three incidents in 30 days: account review

## Shifts and breaks
- 06:00–14:00
- 14:00–22:00
- 22:00–06:00
- Minimum 8 hours rest.
- 60-minute pause per shift.
- At shift end, driver finishes an active trip but receives no new ride after shift end.

## Account standing and ratings
Track:
- no-shows
- late cancellations
- unpaid payments
- abuse
- disputes
Three customer no-shows in 30 days triggers review.
Mutual customer/driver rating: 1–5 stars.

## Payments
- In-app payment only.
- Customer must have a valid payment method.
