# UI / Design Rules

## Quality target
The intended quality benchmark is comparable to mainstream ride-hailing / delivery apps such as Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, and Skip, while keeping the approved OK Let's Go visual identity.

## Logo
- Use the exact approved OK Let's Go logo asset.
- Do not redraw, recolor, or alter the logo unless the owner explicitly requests it.

## Core colours
- Background: #0B1220
- Panels: #131D2E
- Border: #26354D
- Main text: #F8FAFC
- Blue: #2563EB
- Green: #16A34A
- Red: #DC2626

Orange is also used in approved driver/role visuals.

## Phone chrome
Do not add fake:
- time
- Wi-Fi
- battery
- signal
- hamburger menu
- notification bell
unless specifically requested for a particular screen.

## District copy
Display:
`Toronto, Waterloo, Kitchener`

## General interaction rules used in the prototype
- Every demo page should have a Back path.
- Earlier onboarding/demo pages use Next/Back.
- Prototype form fields were intentionally allowed to be optional so the flow could always be tested.
- Buttons/selectors should be interactive in the prototype.
- Do not add an insurance upload field to the driver onboarding flow; the business already expects that information to be handled separately.

## Approved driver Account simplification
Keep the account area relatively simple:
- Edit Profile
- District & Shift
- Help & Support

## Rating/tip
Approved customer final screen concept:
- green stars
- tip presets 5% / 10% / 20%
- wording: `100% of your tip goes to the driver`

## Visual references
See `04-UI-REFERENCES/`.
They are included to show the approved direction and should be used as visual reference, not automatically interpreted as production-ready assets.
